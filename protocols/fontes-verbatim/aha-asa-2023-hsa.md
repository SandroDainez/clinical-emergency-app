## S-01 · Prevenção de ressangramento — PA, reversão, antifibrinolítico

**Localização:** §6 *Medical Measures to Prevent Rebleeding After aSAH*, tabela
*Recommendations for Medical Measures to Prevent Rebleeding After aSAH*, p. **e326**.
**População:** adultos com HSA aneurismática (aneurisma ainda não tratado).

> **rec. 1 · COR 1 · LOE C-EO** — "In patients with aSAH and unsecured aneurysm,
> frequent blood pressure (BP) monitoring and BP control with short-acting
> medication(s) is recommended to avoid severe hypotension, hypertension, and BP
> variability."

> **rec. 2 · COR 1 · LOE C-EO** — "In patients with aSAH who are receiving
> anticoagulants, emergency anticoagulation reversal with appropriate reversal
> agents should be performed to prevent rebleeding."

> **rec. 3 · COR 3: No benefit · LOE A** — "In patients with aSAH, routine use of
> antifibrinolytic therapy is not useful to improve functional outcome."

⚠️ **Achado (E-30):** o controle de PA usa **medicação de ação curta** e o alvo é
**evitar hipotensão/hipertensão/variabilidade** — a guideline **não fixa um alvo
numérico de PAS** (texto de suporte, p. e326: "there is insufficient evidence to
recommend a particular BP target"; menciona que guidelines anteriores sugeriam
PAS <160 mmHg, e que quedas súbitas/profundas devem ser evitadas). Antifibrinolítico
**de rotina não é útil (COR 3: No benefit, LOE A)** — apesar de o ULTRA ter usado
janela curta até o aneurisma ser tratado.

## S-02 · Tratamento do aneurisma roto — TIMING e meta ⚠️ COR 1 <24 h

**Localização:** §7 *Surgical and Endovascular Methods for Treatment of Ruptured
Cerebral Aneurysms*, tabela p. **e327**.
**População:** adultos com HSA aneurismática. **Espécie:** janela temporal + meta +
modalidade.

#### Timing e meta
> **rec. 1 · COR 1 · LOE B-NR** — "For patients with aSAH, surgical or endovascular
> treatment of the ruptured aneurysm should be performed as early as feasible after
> presentation, preferably within 24 hours of onset, to improve outcome."

> **rec. 2 · COR 1 · LOE B-NR** — "For patients with aSAH, complete obliteration of
> the ruptured aneurysm is indicated whenever feasible to reduce the risk of
> rebleeding and retreatment."

> **rec. 5 · COR 1 · LOE B-R** — "For patients with aSAH deemed salvageable with
> depressed level of consciousness due to large intraparenchymal hematoma,
> emergency clot evacuation should be performed to reduce mortality."

> **rec. 6 · COR 1 · LOE C-EO** — "For patients with aSAH, the ruptured aneurysm
> should be evaluated by specialist(s) with endovascular and surgical expertise to
> determine the relative risks and benefits of surgical or endovascular treatment
> according to patient and aneurysm characteristics."

#### Modalidade (clipagem × embolização/coil)
> **rec. 4 · COR 1 · LOE B-R** — "For patients with aSAH from ruptured aneurysms of
> the posterior circulation that are amenable to coiling, coiling is indicated in
> preference to clipping to improve outcome."

> **rec. 9 · COR 1 · LOE A** — "For patients with good-grade aSAH from ruptured
> aneurysms of the anterior circulation equally suitable for both primary coiling
> and clipping, primary coiling is recommended in preference to clipping to improve
> 1-year functional outcome."

> **rec. 7 · COR 2b · LOE B-R** — "For patients >70 years of age with aSAH, the
> superiority of coiling to improve outcome is not well established."

> **rec. 8 · COR 2b · LOE C-LD** — "For patients <40 years of age with aSAH,
> clipping of the ruptured aneurysm might be considered the preferred mode of
> treatment to improve durability of the treatment and outcome."

> **rec. 13 · COR 3: Harm · LOE B-NR** — "For patients with aSAH from ruptured
> saccular aneurysms amenable to either primary coiling or clipping, stents or flow
> diverters should not be used to avoid higher risk of complications."

⚠️⚠️ **Achado (E-30):** tratar o aneurisma **o mais cedo possível, preferencialmente
<24 h (COR 1)**; obliteração **completa** quando factível (COR 1). Modalidade
depende da população: **coiling preferível** na circulação posterior (COR 1) e na
anterior good-grade equally-suitable (COR 1, LOE A); **clipagem** pode ser preferida
em <40 anos (COR 2b). Stents/flow diverters em sacular agudo tratável por coil/clip
**não devem ser usados (COR 3: Harm)**.

## S-03 · Vasoespasmo e DCI — nimodipino, euvolemia, PA, resgate

**Localização:** §8.3 *Management of Cerebral Vasospasm and DCI After aSAH*, tabela
p. **e339**; Figura 3, p. **e340**; dose no texto de suporte p. **e340**.
**População:** adultos com HSA aneurismática. **Espécie:** recomendação + dose (texto
de suporte).

> **rec. 1 · COR 1 · LOE A** — "In patients with aSAH, early initiation of enteral
> nimodipine is beneficial in preventing DCI and improving functional outcomes."

> **rec. 2 · COR 2a · LOE B-NR** — "In patients with aSAH, maintaining euvolemia
> can be beneficial in preventing DCI and improving functional outcomes."

> **rec. 3 · COR 2b · LOE B-NR** — "In patients with aSAH and symptomatic
> vasospasm, elevating systolic BP values may be reasonable to reduce the
> progression and severity of DCI."

> **rec. 4 · COR 2b · LOE B-NR** — "In patients with aSAH and severe vasospasm, use
> of intra-arterial vasodilator therapy may be reasonable to reverse cerebral
> vasospasm and reduce the progression and severity of DCI."

> **rec. 5 · COR 2b · LOE B-NR** — "In patients with aSAH and severe vasospasm,
> cerebral angioplasty may be reasonable to reverse cerebral vasospasm and reduce
> the progression and severity of DCI."

> **rec. 6 · COR 3: No benefit · LOE A** — "In patients with aSAH, routine use of
> statin therapy to improve outcomes is not recommended."

> **rec. 7 · COR 3: No benefit · LOE A** — "In patients with aSAH, routine use of
> intravenous magnesium to improve neurological outcomes is not recommended."

> **rec. 8 · COR 3: Harm · LOE B-R** — "In patients with aSAH at risk of DCI,
> prophylactic hemodynamic augmentation should not be performed to avoid iatrogenic
> patient harm."

#### Dose do nimodipino — §8.3 supportive text, p. e340 (⚠️ TEXTO DE SUPORTE)
> "Nimodipine is a dihydropyridine calcium channel blocker that was approved by the
> US Food and Drug Administration for clinical neurological improvement after aSAH.
> Continued enteral administration at a dose of **60 mg 6 times a day** can be
> beneficial in preventing DCI and improving functional outcome, as originally
> published in the 1983 clinical trial and confirmed in a meta-analysis of 16 trials
> involving 3361 patients."

⚠️⚠️ **Achado (E-30) — a síntese dizia "60 mg 4/4h × 21 dias".** O verbatim primário
diz **"60 mg 6 times a day"** (= 6×/dia = 4/4h, coincide). Mas a **duração de 21
dias NÃO está no texto desta guideline** — vem do ensaio original (Allen, NEJM
1983) e da bula. ⛔ Não afirmar "21 dias" como número desta fonte; se entrar em
tela, marcar a origem (bula/ensaio) em `fontes-br-*` ou registrar como texto de
suporte histórico.

⚠️ **Achado clínico central:** o pilar é **nimodipino enteral precoce (COR 1, LOE
A)** + **euvolemia (COR 2a)**. **Estatina de rotina** e **magnésio IV de rotina**
**não são recomendados (COR 3: No benefit, LOE A)**; **aumento hemodinâmico
profilático é danoso (COR 3: Harm)**. O antigo "Triplo-H profilático" está
formalmente contraindicado.

## S-04 · Monitorização/detecção de vasoespasmo e DCI

**Localização:** §8.2 *Monitoring and Detection of Cerebral Vasospasm and DCI*,
tabela p. **e338**.
**População:** adultos com HSA aneurismática.

> **rec. 1 · COR 2a · LOE B-NR** — "In patients with aSAH with suspected vasospasm
> or limited neurological examination, CTA or CT perfusion (CTP) can be useful to
> detect vasospasm and predict DCI."

> **rec. 2 · COR 2a · LOE B-NR** — "In patients with aSAH, transcranial Doppler
> (TCD) ultrasound monitoring is reasonable to detect vasospasm and predict DCI."

> **rec. 3 · COR 2a · LOE B-NR** — "In patients with high-grade aSAH, continuous
> EEG (cEEG) monitoring can be useful to predict DCI."

⚠️ **Definições (texto de suporte, p. e338):** DCI = "the occurrence of focal
neurological impairment or a decrease of at least 2 points on the GCS", durando ≥1 h,
não atribuível a outras causas. Vasoespasmo/DCI ocorre em ≈30% dos pacientes, entre
os dias **4 e 14** após a HSA.

## S-05 · Hidrocefalia

**Localização:** §8.4 *Management of Hydrocephalus Associated With aSAH*, tabela
p. **e343**.
**População:** adultos com HSA aneurismática.

> **rec. 1 · COR 1 · LOE B-NR** — "In patients with aSAH and acute symptomatic
> hydrocephalus, urgent CSF diversion (EVD and/or lumbar drainage) should be
> performed to improve neurological outcome."

> **rec. 2 · COR 1 · LOE B-NR** — "In patients with aSAH and hydrocephalus who
> require an EVD, implementation and adherence to an EVD bundled protocol that
> addresses insertion, management, education, and monitoring is recommended to
> reduce complication and infection rates."

> **rec. 3 · COR 1 · LOE B-NR** — "In patients with aSAH and associated chronic
> symptomatic hydrocephalus, permanent CSF diversion is recommended to improve
> neurological outcome."

⚠️ **Achado:** hidrocefalia aguda sintomática → **derivação liquórica urgente (EVD
e/ou dreno lombar), COR 1**; hidrocefalia crônica → derivação permanente (COR 1).

## S-06 · Convulsões

**Localização:** §8.5 *Management of Seizures Associated With aSAH*, tabela
p. **e344–e345**.
**População:** adultos com HSA aneurismática.

#### Pacientes que se apresentam SEM convulsão
> **rec. 1 · COR 2a · LOE B-NR** — "In patients with aSAH and either fluctuating
> neurological examination, depressed mental state, ruptured MCA aneurysm,
> high-grade SAH, ICH, hydrocephalus, or cortical infarction, cEEG monitoring is
> reasonable to detect seizures."

> **rec. 2 · COR 2b · LOE B-NR** — "In patients with aSAH and high-seizure-risk
> features (ie, ruptured MCA aneurysm, high-grade SAH, ICH, hydrocephalus, and
> cortical infarction), use of prophylactic antiseizure medication(s) may be
> reasonable to prevent seizures."

> **rec. 3 · COR 3: No benefit · LOE B-R** — "In patients with aSAH without
> high-seizure-risk features (ie, ruptured MCA aneurysm, high-grade SAH, ICH,
> hydrocephalus, and cortical infarction), prophylactic treatment with antiseizure
> medication is not beneficial."

> **rec. 4 · COR 3: Harm · LOE B-NR** — "In patients with aSAH, phenytoin for
> seizure prevention and/or antiseizure prophylaxis is associated with excess
> morbidity and mortality."

#### Pacientes que se apresentam COM convulsão
> **rec. 5 · COR 2a · LOE B-NR** — "In patients with aSAH who present with seizures,
> treatment with antiseizure medications for ≤7 days is reasonable to reduce
> seizure-related complications in the perioperative period."

> **rec. 6 · COR 3: No benefit · LOE B-NR** — "In patients with aSAH without prior
> epilepsy who present with seizures, treatment with antiseizure medications beyond
> 7 days is not effective for reducing future SAH-associated seizure risk."

⚠️ **Achado (E-30):** **fenitoína para prevenção é danosa (COR 3: Harm)** — se for
usar profilaxia em alto risco, ⛔ não usar fenitoína. Quem chega convulsionando:
tratar **≤7 dias** (COR 2a); além de 7 dias não é efetivo (COR 3: No benefit).

## S-07 · Complicações clínicas — volume, TEV, glicemia, febre

**Localização:** §8 *Management of Medical Complications Associated With aSAH*,
tabela p. **e332–e333**.
**População:** adultos com HSA aneurismática.

> **rec. 3 · COR 2a · LOE B-R** — "In patients with aSAH, close monitoring and
> goal-directed treatment of volume status is reasonable to maintain euvolemia."

> **rec. 5 · COR 3: Harm · LOE B-R** — "In patients with aSAH, induction of
> hypervolemia is potentially harmful because of the association with excess
> morbidity."

> **rec. 6 · COR 1 · LOE C-LD** — "In patients with aSAH whose ruptured aneurysm
> has been secured, pharmacological or mechanical venous thromboembolism (VTE)
> prophylaxis is recommended to reduce the risk for VTE."

> **rec. 7 · COR 2a · LOE B-NR** — "In patients with aSAH, effective glycemic
> control, strict hyperglycemia management, and avoidance of hypoglycemia are
> reasonable to improve outcome."

⚠️ **Achado:** manter **euvolemia (COR 2a)**; **hipervolemia induzida é danosa (COR
3: Harm)** — outro prego no caixão do Triplo-H profilático. Tromboprofilaxia só
**depois do aneurisma tratado** (rec. 6, "whose ruptured aneurysm has been
secured").

## S-08 · Sistemas de cuidado

**Localização:** §5 *Hospital Characteristics and Systems of Care*, Top 10 Message
#1/#2; §7 rec. 6 (avaliação por especialista).
**População:** adultos com HSA aneurismática.

⚠️ Transferência para centro com expertise em HSA e neurointensivismo (Top 10 #1);
avaliação precoce, obliteração do aneurisma preferencialmente <24 h (Top 10 #2,
detalhado em **S-02**). O ramo de sistemas entra na superfície de destino/transferência.
