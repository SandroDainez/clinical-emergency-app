# 0 · ESCOPO E POPULAÇÃO

## 0.1 · A quem o documento se aplica — Assumptions, p. 1620

> **1.** "The guidance in this Concise Clinical Guidance is intended for **adult
> patients** with CS noting that pediatric presentations of CS differ and may
> require a modified Society for Cardiovascular Angiography and Intervention
> (SCAI) system."
>
> **2.** "**The principal focus of this document applies to patients hospitalized
> due to CS secondary to AMI (AMI-CS) or HF (HF-CS).**"
>
> **3.** "The guidance is intended for clinicians across a broad array of
> disciplines who routinely evaluate and manage patients in CS in diverse
> clinical settings."
>
> **4.** "The writing committee endorses the evidence-based approach to CS
> diagnosis and management recommended in the 2021 ACC/American Heart
> Association/SCAI Guideline for Coronary Artery Revascularization and in the
> 2022 American Heart Association/ACC/Heart Failure Society of America Guideline
> for the Management of Heart Failure."
>
> **7.** "As new data emerge, they will likely inform the considerations and
> suggestions for clinical practice provided here."

⚠️⚠️ **A Assumption 2 é a fronteira de população deste slot.** O foco principal
é **AMI-CS ou HF-CS**, em paciente **hospitalizado por causa do choque**.

## 0.2 · Definições — §2.2, p. 1620–1621

**CS:**
> "A cardiac disorder that results in both clinical and biochemical evidence of
> sustained tissue hypoperfusion **irrespective of underlying blood pressure**."

⚠️⚠️ *"irrespective of underlying blood pressure"* — converge com a rec. 2 do
**F-32** (*"hypotension is not required to define shock"*) e com a nota de
rodapé do **F-33**. **Três fontes independentes dizendo o mesmo.**

**AMI-CS:**
> "AMI-CS includes patients with CS due to AMI in the presence or absence of
> ST-segment elevation on 12-lead electrocardiography (ie, STEMI and non-STEMI,
> respectively). (…) Note that CS resulting from acute bradyarrhythmias,
> tachyarrhythmias, or advanced heart block, postcardiac arrest, or any other
> complications in the setting of AMI are also classified as AMI-CS."

**HF-CS:**
> "HF-CS is due to CS related to primary myocardial dysfunction ascribed to
> either ischemic or nonischemic etiologies of cardiomyopathy. It may be further
> subcategorized into those with either ***de novo* HF-CS** (ie, acute myocardial
> dysfunction that is known or suspected to be new in onset) vs **acute-on-chronic
> HF-CS** (ie, acute decompensation of chronic or progressive HF with dilated
> cardiomyopathy). (…) HF-CS may be further categorized by the specific etiology
> of the underlying myocardial dysfunction, including—but not limited to—acute
> myocarditis, takotsubo cardiomyopathy, peripartum cardiomyopathy,
> tachycardia-related cardiomyopathy, hypertrophic cardiomyopathy, or
> infiltrative diseases such as cardiac amyloidosis or sarcoidosis, among many
> others."

## 0.3 · ⚠️ As outras etiologias, citadas mas NÃO desenvolvidas — p. 1623

> "the phenotype of CS, which distinguishes between the following etiologies:
> AMI-CS; HF-CS; postcardiotomy CS; and **secondary CS (due to arrhythmias,
> valvular disease, pericardial disease, or other etiologies)**."

⛔ O documento **nomeia** pós-cardiotomia e CS secundário, mas o foco declarado
continua sendo AMI-CS e HF-CS (Assumption 2). ⛔ Não tratar as figuras 7 e 8
como se cobrissem essas outras etiologias.

# 1 · RECONHECIMENTO — §4.1, p. 1622

## 1.1 · O ponto de partida

> "The diagnosis of CS, however, begins with a suspicion of an underlying state
> of inadequate cardiac output in patients at risk. This suspicion can then be
> confirmed using readily obtainable clinical, laboratory, and/or imaging data."

⚠️ E a crítica que o próprio documento faz aos critérios clássicos:

> "Traditionally, for purposes of research and enrollment in clinical trials, the
> criteria used to diagnose CS have included hypotension and end-organ
> hypoperfusion along with evidence of congestion or decreased cardiac output
> (…); however, these criteria dichotomize a dynamic clinical entity across a
> spectrum of illness severity and they also fail to acknowledge the deleterious
> consequences of **normotensive CS** (ie, end-organ hypoperfusion without
> hypotension), which has been associated with increased mortality."

## 1.2 · O que levanta a suspeita — p. 1622

> "Presence of hypotension (defined as systolic blood pressure <90 mm Hg, or mean
> arterial pressure [MAP] <60 mm Hg, or a >30 mm Hg drop from baseline for >30
> minutes), a heart rate >100 beats per minute, and a narrow pulse pressure
> (<25% of systolic blood pressure), in isolation or in combination, should all
> raise suspicion for CS. Physical exam findings of lethargy, confusion, altered
> mental status, cold and sweaty extremities, prolonged capillary refill times
> (>2 s) and reduced urine output (<30 mL/h or <0.5 mL/[kg·h]), even in the
> absence of hypotension, should similarly raise consideration of CS."

## 1.3 · TABELA 1 · "SUSPECT CS" — p. 1622 · mnemônico, **não é escore**

| letra | conteúdo, verbatim |
|---|---|
| **S**ymptoms/Signs | "Altered mental status, confusion, chest pain or pressure, cold and clammy extremities, rapid pulse, low pulse pressure (<25% of SBP), elevated jugular venous pressure, crackles, rales, orthopnea, paroxysmal nocturnal dyspnea, lower extremity edema" |
| **U**rine output | "Oliguria or anuria, <30 mL/h (<0.5 mL/[kg·h])" |
| **S**ustained hypotension | "SBP <90 mm Hg, MAP <65 mm Hg for >30 min or a >30-mm Hg decrease from baseline, or the need for pharmacological or mechanical support to maintain SBP >90 mm Hg" |
| **P**erfusion | "Evaluate markers of end-organ malperfusion, including lactic acid >2 mmol/L, ALT >200 U/L or >3× upper limit of normal, creatinine ≥2× upper limit of normal, pH <7.2, metabolic acidosis without another known cause" |
| **E**CG/**E**chocardiogram | "Evaluate ischemia, including ECG and sonographic evidence of STEMI (regional wall motion abnormalities); evidence of LV or RV dilation and systolic dysfunction; valvular pathology" |
| **C**ongestion | "Presence or absence of congestion based on physical signs and hemodynamics; elucidation of ventricular involvement (LV vs RV)" |
| **T**riage | "Appropriate triage/shock team activation or possible transfer to a higher level of care" |

⚠️⚠️ **Divergência interna do documento, registrada:** o texto corrido da
p. 1622 define hipotensão com **MAP <60 mm Hg**; a Tabela 1, na mesma página,
usa **MAP <65 mm Hg**. Dois números para o mesmo conceito, no mesmo documento.
⛔ Não escolher um por conveniência.

## 1.4 · O eco — §4.1, p. 1622

> "Every patient suspected to be in CS should have a 12-lead electrocardiogram
> and, where available, a transthoracic echocardiogram or point-of-care cardiac
> ultrasound performed by an experienced clinician."

E, decisivo para o desenho do C2:

> "It is important to highlight that the initial suspicion and diagnosis of CS
> **does not require invasive hemodynamics**; however, invasive hemodynamics are
> often useful in elucidating the ventricular involvement and congestive profile
> of the CS patient and may inform therapeutic decision making."

⚠️ Convergente com a decisão já tomada no C2: **o eco é oferta, nunca portão**.

# 2 · PERFIL HEMODINÂMICO — §4.4, p. 1626–1627

> "Hemodynamics enable the bedside clinician to elucidate the congestion profile
> (**LV dominant, RV dominant, or biventricular**), which have been characterized
> by adverse outcomes in both AMI-CS and HF-CS."

> "LV dominant congestion in CS is often characterized by an elevated pulmonary
> capillary wedge pressure or LV end-diastolic pressure **>15 mm Hg**. In
> contrast, RV dominant congestion in CS is accompanied by a relatively normal
> pulmonary capillary wedge pressure in the setting of an elevated right atrial
> pressure (right atrial or central venous pressure **>15 mm Hg**). Biventricular
> congestive profiles suggest elevation in both right atrial and pulmonary
> capillary wedge pressures."

> "Hemodynamic parameters associated with adverse mortality in CS include low
> MAP, elevated right atrial pressure, an elevated right atrial pressure to
> pulmonary capillary wedge pressure ratio (**>0.6 mm Hg**), and a reduced
> pulmonary artery pulsatility index"

⚠️ O *"(>0.6 mm Hg)"* traz unidade num índice adimensional — **errata do
documento**, transcrita como está.

> "The writing committee suggests integrating invasive hemodynamic assessment
> with noninvasive cardiac imaging (ie, echocardiography or point-of-care
> ultrasound whenever available) to more precisely characterize the phenotype of
> CS patients."

⚠️ **PAPi** — critérios diferentes por população: *"Criteria for low PAPi values
differ between AMI-CS (<0.9 mm Hg) and HF patients undergoing LVAD implantation
(<1.85 mm Hg)."*

# 3 · ⚠️⚠️⚠️ §4.5 · MANEJO FARMACOLÓGICO — p. 1627–1629

## 3.1 · O objetivo declarado

> "In CS, the goal of pharmacological therapies is to mitigate congestion
> (whenever present), optimize cardiac output, and enhance perfusion to vital
> organs."

## 3.2 · Congestão primeiro

> "Whenever a congestive phenotype exists, it should be addressed through
> intravenous loop diuretics, augmentation with thiazide diuretics, and renal
> replacement therapy for ultrafiltration if congestion is refractory to medical
> management. Failure to address congestion may lead to microcirculatory ischemia
> and damage to multiple organs, including the kidneys, liver, and
> gastrointestinal tract."

## 3.3 · ⚠️ A advertência de dose e duração

> "These drugs are commonly used in clinical practice for the management of CS
> but some have been associated with increased adverse effects, including
> arrhythmias, and increased myocardial oxygen consumption. Therefore, the
> writing committee advises that **vasoactive medications be used in CS at the
> lowest possible dose to support adequate perfusion and for the shortest
> possible duration**."

## 3.4 · As classes, e o que cada uma faz

> "Inopressors (eg, dopamine, norepinephrine, and epinephrine) increase cardiac
> output while increasing systemic vascular resistance, whereas inodilators (eg,
> milrinone or dobutamine) increase cardiac output and reduce afterload through
> systemic vasodilation. Vasodilators decrease preload and/or LV afterload
> through reduction in systemic vascular resistance, thereby potentially
> augmenting cardiac output. Pure vasopressors increase MAP by increasing
> systemic vascular resistance."

> "Chronotropes, on the other hand, increase cardiac output by predominantly
> increasing the heart rate."

## 3.6 · ⚠️⚠️ A RESSALVA DE PRIMEIRA LINHA — p. 1629

> "A Cochrane analysis found **insufficient evidence to support the superiority
> of a particular inotrope or vasodilating agent in CS**, especially with respect
> to a mortality benefit."

> "**Although there is no clear consensus regarding the choice of first-line
> vasoactive agent, the writing committee agrees that norepinephrine is a
> reasonable first choice for most patients with CS who are hypotensive.**"

⚠️⚠️ Ler as duas frases juntas, e nunca a segunda sozinha. A noradrenalina é
*"a reasonable first choice"* **para quem está hipotenso**, num campo em que o
próprio documento declara **não haver consenso claro**. ⛔ Isso ⛔ não é
"noradrenalina é a droga do choque cardiogênico".

## 3.7 · DOREMI — dobutamina × milrinona — p. 1628–1629

> "The Dobutamine Compared with Milrinone (DOREMI) trial evaluated dobutamine vs
> milrinone in 192 CS patients in a single quaternary care academic center,
> ranging from SCAI B through E and excluding patients with cardiac arrest, and
> found no difference with respect to the primary composite endpoint of
> in-hospital death from any cause, resuscitated cardiac arrest, receipt of a
> cardiac transplant or mechanical circulatory support, nonfatal myocardial
> infarction, transient ischemic attack or stroke diagnosed by a neurologist, or
> initiation of renal replacement therapy."

## 3.8 · ⚠️ As cautelas nomeadas — p. 1629

> "Given the heterogenous nature of CS and the various physiological
> derangements, several vasoactive medications may be attempted to normalize a CS
> patient's hemodynamic and/or metabolic profile. **Inodilators or vasodilators
> may be considered in normotensive CS**, especially in patients with increased
> systemic vascular resistance. **Intravenous milrinone, given its relatively long
> half-life and renal excretion, should be judiciously used in patients with
> worsening renal function. Chronotropes might be trialed in bradycardia-induced
> CS. Since pure vasopressors (ie, phenylephrine) can cause reflex bradycardia
> and reduce cardiac output in CS, their use as a single first-line continuous
> intravenous medication is strongly discouraged.**"

> "Level 2 and 3 CS hospital centers should pursue consultation and consider
> potential transfer to a Level 1 CS hospital center when patients are refractory
> to the initial pharmacological therapy for consideration of tMCS support and/or
> advanced therapies."

# ⚠️⚠️⚠️ §APLICABILIDADE — o que serve ao AVC, e o que não serve

## O fato que governa esta seção

⛔ **O documento não trata de AVC como população.** A palavra *stroke* aparece
apenas como **desfecho** — no endpoint composto do DOREMI (*"transient ischemic
attack or stroke diagnosed by a neurologist"*), como complicação de transferência
e como item de "prognóstico neurológico" na Figura 4.

⛔ **Não existe uma linha sobre choque cardiogênico EM paciente com AVC agudo.**

## A armadilha antecipada pelo autor, confirmada pelo documento

> **Assumption 2:** *"The principal focus of this document applies to patients
> **hospitalized due to CS** secondary to AMI (AMI-CS) or HF (HF-CS)."*

⚠️⚠️ O paciente do C2 é o oposto: **hospitalizado por AVC**, que **desenvolve**
choque. ⛔ Não é "hospitalizado por causa do choque", e ⛔ não é necessariamente
AMI-CS nem HF-CS.

➜ A fonte pode estar inteiramente correta e a aplicação ao fluxo do AVC ainda
assim exigir qualificação. **Fidelidade e aplicabilidade são eixos
independentes.**

## O que é candidato ao AVC, e sob qual etiqueta

| conteúdo | por que é candidato |
|---|---|
| **definição de CS** — hipoperfusão sustentada *irrespective of blood pressure* | é **definição**, não conduta, e converge com F-32 rec. 2 e F-33 |
| **o eco não é pré-requisito** para suspeitar nem diagnosticar | converge com a decisão já tomada no C2 |
| **dose mais baixa possível, pelo menor tempo possível** | princípio de segurança, não meta de população |
| **as três cautelas nomeadas** (milrinona na disfunção renal; fenilefrina isolada fortemente desencorajada; cronotrópico na bradicardia) | são **contraindicações**, e contraindicação erra para o lado seguro |
| **Tabela 2**, como faixas de agente | ⚠️ **candidata com etiqueta** — ver abaixo |

## ⛔ O que NÃO é transportável

| conteúdo | por quê |
|---|---|
| **MAP >60–65 mmHg** como meta | ⛔ meta **de choque cardiogênico**. Proibição 1 do contrato. E para o AVC valem a rec. 38 do F-32 (individualizar) + F-05 (corrigir), **sem número** |
| **Figuras 7 e 8** (algoritmos por estágio SCAI) | ⛔ são de **AMI-CS** e **HF-CS**, com tMCS e centro de referência |
| **estratégia de reperfusão** | ⛔ é de AMI-CS. Proibição 2 do contrato |
| **Tabela 3** (reavaliação estruturada) | ⛔ é **de UTI**, dito no título. Proibição 3 do contrato |
| **níveis 1/2/3 de centro** | ⛔ o próprio documento diz que **não há consenso** sobre essa classificação |
| **SCAI B–E** como estadiamento operacional | ⚠️ é classificação de gravidade validada em população de CS; entra, se entrar, como **classificação**, e ⛔ nunca como gatilho de droga |

## ⚠️ A etiqueta obrigatória da Tabela 2

Se as faixas da Tabela 2 forem para a tela, a frase que o médico lê tem de
carregar **três coisas**, e nenhuma é opcional:

1. é **consenso de especialistas sem graduação formal** — o documento não emite
   COR, LOE nem GRADE;
2. o próprio documento declara que **não há consenso claro** sobre o agente de
   primeira linha, e a Cochrane citada achou **evidência insuficiente** de
   superioridade;
3. a noradrenalina é *"a reasonable first choice"* **para quem está hipotenso**
   — e ⛔ não "a droga do choque cardiogênico".

⚠️ E a população: **AMI-CS e HF-CS hospitalizados por causa do choque**. Aplicar
a um AVC que desenvolve choque cardiogênico é **analogia de mecanismo**, decisão
do autor, e ⛔ nunca leitura direta da fonte.

## D-2 · ⚠️⚠️ A armadilha de extração que o autor previu

O autor alertou: *"esse tipo de tabela é exatamente onde `mcg` pode virar `mg`
numa extração automática"*. **Ele estava certo, e aconteceu.**

A camada de texto do PDF devolve, para **todas** as linhas da Tabela 2:

```
0.05-1 mg/kg/min      ← o PDF impresso mostra μg/kg/min
25-200 mg/min         ← o PDF impresso mostra μg/min
```

⛔ O glifo **μ** é extraído como **m**. Aceitar a extração teria multiplicado
**toda** a tabela por **mil**.

⚠️⚠️ **E a conferência a 400 DPI encontrou um segundo erro — meu.** Na primeira
leitura, da página inteira em baixa resolução, eu li **dobutamina 2–20
μg/kg/min**. Ampliada, a tabela diz **2–10 μg/kg/min**. A extração de texto
estava **certa no dígito** e errada na unidade; minha leitura visual estava
certa na unidade e errada no dígito.

➜ **Regra que fica:** conferir tabela de dose por **duas rotas independentes**
— render em alta resolução **e** camada de texto — e investigar toda
discrepância. Nenhuma das duas sozinha é confiável.

## D-3 · Unidade em índice adimensional

p. 1627: *"an elevated right atrial pressure to pulmonary capillary wedge
pressure ratio (>0.6 mm Hg)"*. Uma **razão** entre duas pressões é
adimensional. Errata do original.
