**p. 1972 · *Introduction*** — ⛔ texto de escopo, ⛔ **⛔ não** recomendação

> "The scope of these guidelines is limited to adult patients covering diagnosis
> and monitoring of shock but not pharmacological treatments. Fluid therapy is
> only addressed through indices assessing and monitoring fluid responsiveness,
> while other aspects of the fluid prescription (choice of resuscitation fluids,
> or volume of resuscitation fluids) have been addressed in other
> recommendations of ESICM. Vasopressors are addressed only in the context of
> the effect on arterial pressure."

⛔ ⛔ Consequência ⛔ **⛔ direta**: ⛔ **⛔ escolha de vasopressor, dose, titulação,
tipo e volume de fluido ⛔ NÃO estão aqui.** ⛔ São ⛔ **⛔ lacuna de fonte** —

## ⚠️ Como ler os graus

⛔ A diretriz usa ⛔ **⛔ três naturezas diferentes**, ⛔ e ⛔ misturá-las ⛔ seria
⛔ inventar força:

| natureza | o que é |
|---|---|
| **Ungraded definition** | ⛔ definição, ⛔ **⛔ não** conduta |

# 1 · RECONHECIMENTO DO CHOQUE

**Domain 1 · Question 1.1 · p. 1975 · Fig. 1, p. 1976**

### 1.1 · Definição — rec. 1 · **Ungraded definition** / Ungraded evidence / Strong agreement

> "Shock is defined as a life-threatening acute circulatory failure characterized
> by decreased tissue perfusion, leading to inadequate oxygen delivery and/or
> oxygen utilization to meet cellular metabolic demands."

### 1.2 · Sinais típicos — rec. 2 · **UGPS** / Ungraded evidence / Strong agreement

> "The typical features are hypotension, tachycardia, and signs of hypoperfusion,
> such as abnormal skin perfusion, decreased urine output, and altered mental
> status. **Although hypotension is commonly present, it is not required to
> define shock.**"

⚠️⚠️ ⛔ **⛔ É ⛔ ESTA ⛔ FRASE** ⛔ que fecha ⛔ o buraco ⛔ do módulo: ⛔ o app
⛔ **⛔ não precisa** ⛔ de um limiar de PA ⛔ para reconhecer choque, ⛔ e
⛔ **⛔ não pode** ⛔ exigir um.

### 1.3 · Lactato — rec. 3 · **UGPS** / Ungraded evidence / Strong agreement

> "Lactate levels are typically increased (>2 mmol/L) in shock states."

### ⛔ Expert opinion (p. 1977) — ⛔ **⛔ NÃO é recomendação**

> "(…) while hypotension is common in shock, its absence does not rule out severe
> tissue hypoperfusion. We recommend a multimodal approach to accurately identify
> and assess patients with shock."

# 2 · REAVALIAÇÃO DA PERFUSÃO

**Domain 1 · Question 1.2 · p. 1978** — ⛔ recs. 4 a 8, ⛔ **todas UGPS** /
Ungraded evidence / Strong agreement

**Rec. 4 — avaliação seriada**
> "Serial assessment of tissue perfusion should be performed to follow shock
> evolution and to help assess the underlying pattern and the adequacy of CO and
> vascular function."

**Rec. 5 — pele: CRT, temperatura, *mottling***
> "Monitoring skin perfusion should be performed using the assessment of CRT and
> this could be complemented with the assessment of skin temperature and
> mottling."

**Rec. 6 — ScvO₂ (⛔ exige cateter venoso central)**
> "In patients with a central venous catheter, serial measurements of (central)
> venous oxygen saturation (S(c)vO₂) should be performed."

**Rec. 7 — Pv-aCO₂ (⛔ exige CVC **e** cateter arterial)**
> "In patients with a central venous catheter and an arterial catheter, serial
> measurement of the veno-arterial difference in carbon dioxide partial pressure
> (Pv-aCO₂) should be performed."

**Rec. 8 — razão Pv-aCO₂/Ca-vO₂ · *"one may consider"***
> "In patients with a central venous catheter and an arterial catheter, one may
> consider performing serial measurements of the ratio of Pv-aCO₂ over the
> arterio-venous difference in oxygen content (Pv-aCO₂/Ca-vO₂)."

### Microcirculação — Question 1.3 · rec. 9 · **UGPS** · p. 1979
> "When feasible, the assessment of microcirculation may be considered as an
> adjunct to comprehensive hemodynamic evaluation."

### ⛔ Frequência — ⛔ *Expert opinion*, p. 1979 · ⛔ **⛔ não é recomendação**
> "There is no evidence in the literature to specify the frequency with which
> tissue perfusion assessment should be performed. This assessment should at
> least occur when hemodynamic variables such as blood pressure deteriorate, and
> following therapeutic interventions aimed to modify regional perfusion in order
> to measure their effectiveness."

⚠️ ⛔ E, ⛔ no mesmo lugar: ⛔ *"normalization of lactate levels should not be used
as a target ⛔ but viewed as a consequence of the adequacy of overall treatment."*

# 3 · PRESSÃO ARTERIAL

## 3a · Quando e como monitorar — Question 3.3 · p. 1993 · ⛔ ambas **UGPS**

**Rec. 36**
> "Arterial pressure should be monitored in patients with shock."

**Rec. 37**
> "Arterial pressure should be monitored with an arterial catheter in shock that
> is not responsive to initial therapy and/or requiring vasopressor infusion."

## 3b · Metas — Question 3.4 · p. 1994 · ⛔ **⛔ TODAS UGPS** / Strong agreement

**Rec. 38 — ⛔ individualizar ⛔ é a regra-mãe**
> "The target blood pressure should be individualized during resuscitation of
> patients with shock."

**Rec. 39** — > "An initial MAP of 65–70 mmHg should be targeted in patients with **septic shock**."

**Rec. 40** — > "A higher MAP target may be considered in patients with **septic shock and a history of chronic arterial hypertension** who show clinical improvement with higher blood pressure."

**Rec. 41** — > "A higher MAP target may be considered in patients with **septic shock with high CVP values** who show clinical improvement with higher blood pressure."

**Rec. 42** — > "Lower MAP targets may be considered in patients with **traumatic hemorrhagic shock and uncontrolled bleeding in the absence of traumatic brain injury**."

**Rec. 43 — ⛔ o único alvo NEURO da diretriz, ⛔ e ⛔ ele é de TCE**
> "In the initial phase following trauma, a target systolic arterial pressure of
> 80–90 mmHg (MAP 50–60 mmHg) should be used until major bleeding has been
> stopped **when there is no clinical evidence of traumatic brain injury and coma
> (Glasgow Coma Score ≤ 8)**. **In traumatic brain injury (Glasgow Coma Score ≤ 8),
> we recommend targeting an initial mean arterial pressure ≥80 mmHg.**"

**Rec. 44** — > "Targeting an initial MAP of ≥ 65 mmHg may be considered in patients with **cardiogenic shock**."

### ⚠️⚠️⚠️ ⛔ A ADVERTÊNCIA QUE O AUTOR PEDIU, ⛔ E ⛔ ELA SE SUSTENTA NO TEXTO

⛔ ⛔ **⛔ Nenhuma** ⛔ das metas ⛔ é ⛔ para ⛔ **⛔ AVC isquêmico**. ⛔ Elas são
⛔ **⛔ por tipo de choque**: séptico, hemorrágico, cardiogênico, ⛔ e ⛔ **⛔ TCE**.

⛔ ⛔ Transportar ⛔ o `MAP 65–70` ⛔ do choque séptico ⛔ para o AVC ⛔ seria
⛔ inventar. ⛔ Transportar ⛔ o `MAP ≥80` ⛔ do **TCE** ⛔ para o AVC ⛔ **⛔ também
seria** — ⛔ TCE ⛔ e ⛔ AVC isquêmico ⛔ são ⛔ populações diferentes, ⛔ e ⛔ a
diretriz ⛔ **⛔ não faz** ⛔ essa ponte.

⚠️ ⛔ O que ⛔ **⛔ vale para o AVC** ⛔ é ⛔ a **rec. 38** (individualizar) ⛔ mais
⛔ a fonte-mãe **F-05** (⛔ corrigir hipotensão e hipovolemia, ⛔ COR 1).

## 3c · Pressão venosa central — p. 1989 (Fig. 5) · ⛔ **UGPS**
> "Central venous pressure should be measured in patients with shock who have a
> central venous catheter."
> "A pre-specified CVP value should not be targeted during the resuscitation of
> patients with shock."

# 4 · RESPONSIVIDADE A FLUIDOS

## 4a · Quando avaliar — Question 2.1 · p. 1980 · ⛔ **UGPS**

**Rec. 10**
> "In patients with persistent shock after initial fluid resuscitation, fluid
> responsiveness should be assessed before continuing fluid resuscitation."

**Rec. 11**
> "The potential benefit of fluid administration, predicted by the assessment of
> fluid responsiveness, should be weighed against the potential risk of fluid
> administration."

## 4b · Risco da infusão — Question 2.2 · rec. 12 · p. 1983 · ⛔ **UGPS**
> "The risk of harm from fluid administration could be assessed using markers such
> as intravascular filling pressures, intra-abdominal pressure, extravascular lung
> water (EVLW), pulmonary vascular permeability index (PVPI), venous excess
> ultrasound (VExUS) grading, the ratio of the arterial oxygen partial pressure
> over the inspired oxygen fraction (PaO₂/FiO₂) ratio, or lung ultrasound score."

## 4c · Prova de volume — Question 2.3 · p. 1984

**Rec. 13 · ⛔ Ungraded definition / ⛔ Low certainty of evidence / Strong agreement**
> "A fluid challenge is defined as a bolus of 200 to 500 mL given over 5–10 min
> while evaluating its effects."

**Rec. 14 · UGPS** — > "One may consider assessing the effects of a fluid bolus on CO and, when not available, on pulse pressure rather than MAP."

**Rec. 15 · UGPS** — > "The effectiveness of a fluid bolus in improving tissue perfusion should be evaluated by considering changes in variables such as CRT, skin mottling, S(c)vO₂, carbon dioxide partial pressure (pCO₂)-derived variables, and lactate."

## 4d · Como avaliar — Question 2.4 · p. 1985 · ⛔ **⛔ AQUI HÁ GRADE DE VERDADE**

| # | recomendação | força / certeza |
|---|---|---|
| **16** | "We recommend using **dynamic variables over static markers of preload** for predicting fluid responsiveness, when applicable." | **Strong** / High |
| **17** | "We recommend the **passive leg raising (PLR)** test to assess fluid responsiveness in mechanically ventilated patients in shock, **with and without spontaneous breathing activity**." | **Strong** / High |
| **18** | "We recommend the **end-expiratory occlusion test** as an alternative to PLR test in mechanically ventilated patients in shock **without** spontaneous breathing activity." | **Strong** / Moderate |
| **19** | *tidal volume challenge* — "We are **unable to provide recommendations**… due to paucity and/or heterogeneity of available data." | **No recommendation** / Low |
| **20** | "We recommend **pulse pressure variation (PPV)**… without spontaneous breathing activity **and a tidal volume ≥ 8 mL/kg**." | **Strong** / High |
| **21** | "We **suggest against** the use of PPV alone… **with** spontaneous breathing activity and a tidal volume **< 8 mL/kg**." | **Suggest against** / Moderate |
| **22** | "We suggest using **stroke volume variation (SVV)**… without spontaneous breathing activity and a tidal volume ≥ 8 mL/kg." | **Weak** / Moderate |
| **23** | PPV com respiração espontânea | **No recommendation** / Low |
| **24** | SVV com respiração espontânea | **No recommendation** / Low |
| **25** | *mini-fluid challenge* | **No recommendation** / Low |
| **26** | "We **suggest against** the use of changes in **IVC diameter alone**… to assess fluid responsiveness." | **Suggest against** / Moderate · ⛔ *Weak agreement* |

# 5 · DÉBITO CARDÍACO E ECOCARDIOGRAFIA

## 5a · Quando medir CO — Question 3.1 · p. 1987 · ⛔ **UGPS**

**Rec. 27** — > "CO and/or stroke volume should be monitored in patients who **do not respond to initial therapy** to assess the type of shock, evaluate hemodynamic status, and determine therapeutic response."

**Rec. 28** — > "Frequent reevaluation of CO may be considered."

**Rec. 29** — > "When CO is monitored, its adequacy should be interpreted by evaluating organ function, tissue oxygenation, metabolism, and perfusion."

## 5b · Como — Question 3.2 · p. 1991

**Recs. 30 a 33 · UGPS** — termodiluição transpulmonar / PAC / dispositivos menos invasivos.

**Rec. 34 · ⛔ Weak recommendation / ⛔ Low certainty / Strong agreement** — ⛔ **⛔ o eco**
