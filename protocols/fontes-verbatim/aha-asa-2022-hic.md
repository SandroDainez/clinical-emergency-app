## H-01 · PA aguda — meta e alvo pressórico na HIC leve a moderada

**Localização:** §5.1 *Acute BP Lowering*, tabela *Recommendations for Acute BP
Lowering*, p. **e297–e298**.
**População:** adultos com HIC espontânea. **Espécie:** meta pressórica + janela +
recomendação. **Fonte:** ramo HIC (complementar à fonte-mãe isquêmica).

> **rec. 1 · COR 2a · LOE B-NR** — "In patients with spontaneous ICH requiring
> acute BP lowering, careful titration to ensure continuous smooth and sustained
> control of BP, avoiding peaks and large variability in SBP, can be beneficial
> for improving functional outcomes."

> **rec. 2 · COR 2a · LOE C-LD** — "In patients with spontaneous ICH in whom acute
> BP lowering is considered, initiating treatment within 2 hours of ICH onset and
> reaching target within 1 hour can be beneficial to reduce the risk of HE and
> improve functional outcome."

> **rec. 3 · COR 2b · LOE B-R** — "In patients with spontaneous ICH of mild to
> moderate severity presenting with SBP between 150 and 220 mm Hg, acute lowering
> of SBP to a target of 140 mm Hg with the goal of maintaining in the range of
> 130 to 150 mm Hg is safe and may be reasonable for improving functional
> outcomes."

> **rec. 4 · COR 2b · LOE C-LD** — "In patients with spontaneous ICH presenting
> with large or severe ICH or those requiring surgical decompression, the safety
> and efficacy of intensive BP lowering are not well established."

> **rec. 5 · COR 3: Harm · LOE B-R** — "In patients with spontaneous ICH of mild
> to moderate severity presenting with SBP >150 mm Hg, acute lowering of SBP to
> <130 mm Hg is potentially harmful."

⚠️ **Achado (E-30):** o alvo é **140 mm Hg** mantendo faixa **130–150**; descer
**<130** na HIC leve-moderada com PAS >150 é **potencialmente danoso (COR 3)**. A
faixa 150–220 é a população estudada, ⛔ não um "gatilho para tratar tudo acima de
150". HIC grande/grave ou com necessidade de descompressão: eficácia/segurança do
controle intensivo **não estabelecidas** (rec. 4) — ⛔ não extrapolar a meta.

## H-02 · Reversão de anticoagulação — princípio geral e por agente

**Localização:** §5.2.1 *Anticoagulant-Related Hemorrhage*, tabela
*Recommendations for Anticoagulant-Related Hemorrhage*, p. **e300**; doses
operacionais na **Figura 2, p. e301**.
**População:** adultos com HIC espontânea associada a anticoagulante.
**Espécie:** recomendação (agente de reversão) + doses (figura).

#### Princípio geral
> **rec. 1 · COR 1 · LOE C-LD** — "In patients with anticoagulant-associated
> spontaneous ICH, anticoagulation should be discontinued immediately and rapid
> reversal of anticoagulation should be performed as soon as possible after
> diagnosis of spontaneous ICH to improve survival."

#### Antagonistas da vitamina K (VKA / varfarina)
> **rec. 2 · COR 1 · LOE B-R** — "In patients with VKA-associated spontaneous ICH
> and INR ≥2.0, 4-factor (4-F) prothrombin complex concentrate (PCC) is
> recommended in preference to fresh-frozen plasma (FFP) to achieve rapid
> correction of INR and limit HE."

> **rec. 3 · COR 1 · LOE C-LD** — "In patients with VKA-associated spontaneous
> ICH, intravenous vitamin K should be administered directly after coagulation
> factor replacement (PCC or other) to prevent later increase in INR and
> subsequent HE."

> **rec. 4 · COR 2b · LOE C-LD** — "In patients with VKA-associated spontaneous
> ICH with INR of 1.3 to 1.9, it may be reasonable to use PCC to achieve rapid
> correction of INR and limit HE."

#### Anticoagulantes orais diretos (DOAC)
> **rec. 5 · COR 2a · LOE B-NR** — "In patients with direct factor Xa
> inhibitor–associated spontaneous ICH, andexanet alfa is reasonable to reverse
> the anticoagulant effect of factor Xa inhibitors."

> **rec. 6 · COR 2a · LOE B-NR** — "In patients with dabigatran-associated
> spontaneous ICH, idarucizumab is reasonable to reverse the anticoagulant effect
> of dabigatran."

> **rec. 7 · COR 2b · LOE B-NR** — "In patients with direct factor Xa
> inhibitor–associated spontaneous ICH, a 4-F PCC or activated PCC (aPCC) may be
> considered to improve hemostasis."

> **rec. 8 · COR 2b · LOE C-LD** — "In patients with dabigatran- or factor Xa
> inhibitor–associated spontaneous ICH, when the DOAC agent was taken within the
> previous few hours, activated charcoal may be reasonable to prevent absorption
> of the DOAC."

> **rec. 9 · COR 2b · LOE C-LD** — "In patients with dabigatran-associated
> spontaneous ICH, when idarucizumab is not available, aPCC or PCCs may be
> considered to improve hemostasis."

> **rec. 10 · COR 2b · LOE C-LD** — "In patients with dabigatran-associated
> spontaneous ICH, when idarucizumab is not available, renal replacement therapy
> (RRT) may be considered to reduce dabigatran concentration."

#### Heparinas
> **rec. 11 · COR 2a · LOE C-LD** — "In patients with unfractionated heparin
> (UFH)–associated spontaneous ICH, intravenous protamine is reasonable to reverse
> the anticoagulant effect of heparin."

> **rec. 12 · COR 2b · LOE C-LD** — "In patients with low-molecular-weight heparin
> (LMWH)–associated spontaneous ICH, intravenous protamine may be considered to
> partially reverse the anticoagulant effect of heparin."

#### Doses — Figura 2, p. e301 (⚠️ FIGURA, não corpo COR/LOE)
> "Patients with ICH on anticoagulation → Discontinue anticoagulation therapy
> immediately. Rapid reversal should be performed as soon as possible. (Class 1)"
>
> **VITAMIN K ANTAGONISTS** — INR 1.3–1.9: "4-F PCC 10–20 IU/kg (Class 2b)" · INR
> ≥2.0: "4-F PCC 25–50 IU/kg (Class 1)" · "IV Vitamin K (Class 1)".
>
> **DABIGATRAN** — "Activated charcoal if DOAC <2 hrs (potential efficacy up to
> 8 hrs) (Class 2b)"; "Is Idarucizumab available? YES → Idarucizumab (Class 2a);
> NO → PCCs or aPCC and/or renal replacement therapy (Class 2b)".
>
> **FACTOR Xa-INHIBITORS** — "Is andexanet alfa available? YES → Andexanet alfa
> (Class 2a); NO → 4 Factor PCC or aPCC (Class 2b)".
>
> **HEPARINS** — Unfractionated Heparin → "Protamine (Class 2a)"; Low Molecular
> Weight Heparin → "Protamine (Class 2b)".

⚠️ **Achado (E-30) — idarucizumabe 5 g NÃO está nesta guideline.** A dose de
**5 g** que a síntese trazia aparece no *Recommendation-Specific Supportive Text*
apenas descrevendo um estudo ("idarucizumab 5 g (administered as two 2.5-g
boluses)", texto de suporte, p. e302), e é a dose de bula — **não** é número da
recomendação. Andexanet: o texto de suporte (p. e302) diz "The recommended dosing
of andexanet alfa depends on the specific factor Xa inhibitor and the time since
last dose" — ⛔ não há número fixo na guideline. Protamina UFH: texto de suporte,
p. e303 — "Intravenous protamine should not exceed 50 mg/10 min".

⚠️ **Adaptação brasileira:** andexanet alfa e idarucizumabe têm disponibilidade
restrita no Brasil → os ramos "NO / not available" (aPCC, 4F-PCC, carvão ativado,
RRT) são operacionalmente relevantes. Registrar em `fontes-br-*` se virar conduta
de tela com marca/apresentação nacional.

## H-03 · Hemorragia associada a antiplaquetário

**Localização:** §5.2.2 *Antiplatelet-Related Hemorrhage*, p. **e304**.
**População:** adultos com HIC espontânea em uso de antiplaquetário.

> **rec. 1 · COR 2b · LOE C-LD** — "For patients with spontaneous ICH being
> treated with aspirin and who require emergency neurosurgery, platelet
> transfusion might be considered to reduce postoperative bleeding and mortality."

> **rec. 2 · COR 2b · LOE C-LD** — "For patients with spontaneous ICH being
> treated with antiplatelet agents, the effectiveness of desmopressin with or
> without platelet transfusions to reduce the expansion of the hematoma is
> uncertain."

> **rec. 3 · COR 3: Harm · LOE B-R** — "For patients with spontaneous ICH being
> treated with aspirin and not scheduled for emergency surgery, platelet
> transfusions are potentially harmful and should not be administered."

⚠️ **Achado (E-30):** transfusão de plaquetas na HIC por aspirina **fora do
contexto de neurocirurgia de emergência é potencialmente danosa (COR 3: Harm)** —
o oposto do reflexo de "reverter o antiplaquetário".

## H-04 · Tratamentos hemostáticos gerais (rFVIIa, TXA)

**Localização:** §5.2.3 *General Hemostatic Treatments*, p. **e305**.
**População:** adultos com HIC espontânea.

> **rec. 1 · COR 2b · LOE B-R** — "In patients with spontaneous ICH (with or
> without the spot sign), the effectiveness of recombinant factor VIIa to improve
> functional outcome is unclear."

> **rec. 2 · COR 2b · LOE B-R** — "In patients with spontaneous ICH (with or
> without the spot sign, black hole sign, or blend sign), the effectiveness of TXA
> to improve functional outcome is not well established."

⚠️ **Achado:** nem rFVIIa nem TXA têm eficácia estabelecida na HIC (ambos COR 2b,
"unclear"/"not well established"). ⛔ Não apresentar como conduta hemostática de
rotina.

## H-05 · Glicemia — monitorização e alvos

**Localização:** §5.3.5 *Glucose Management*, p. **e313**.
**População:** adultos com HIC espontânea. **Espécie:** recomendação + alvos.

> **rec. 1 · COR 1 · LOE C-LD** — "In patients with spontaneous ICH, monitoring
> serum glucose is recommended to reduce the risk of hyperglycemia and
> hypoglycemia."

> **rec. 2 · COR 1 · LOE C-LD** — "In patients with spontaneous ICH, treating
> hypoglycemia (<40–60 mg/dL, 2.2–3.3 mmol/L) is recommended to reduce mortality."

> **rec. 3 · COR 2a · LOE C-LD** — "In patients with spontaneous ICH, treating
> moderate to severe hyperglycemia (>180–200 mg/dL, >10.0–11.1 mmol/L) is
> reasonable to improve outcomes."

⚠️ **Achado:** os alvos (<40–60 e >180–200 mg/dL) estão **no corpo da recomendação**
(diferente do isquêmico, cujo alvo vem por adaptação BR — F-06/F-18). Registrar
como número sustentado pela fonte.

## H-07 · Convulsões e antiepilépticos

**Localização:** §5.4 *Seizures and Antiseizure Drugs*, p. **e315**.
**População:** adultos com HIC espontânea.

> **rec. 1 · COR 1 · LOE C-LD** — "In patients with spontaneous ICH, impaired
> consciousness, and confirmed electrographic seizures, antiseizure drugs should
> be administered to reduce morbidity."

> **rec. 2 · COR 1 · LOE C-EO** — "In patients with spontaneous ICH and clinical
> seizures, antiseizure drugs are recommended to improve functional outcomes and
> prevent brain injury from prolonged recurrent seizures."

> **rec. 3 · COR 2a · LOE C-LD** — "In patients with spontaneous ICH and
> unexplained abnormal or fluctuating mental status or suspicion of seizures,
> continuous electroencephalography (≥24 hours) is reasonable to diagnose
> electrographic seizures and epileptiform discharges."

> **rec. 4 · COR 3: No Benefit · LOE B-NR** — "In patients with spontaneous ICH
> without evidence of seizures, prophylactic antiseizure medication is not
> beneficial to improve functional outcomes, long-term seizure control, or
> mortality."

⚠️ **Achado:** trata-se convulsão **confirmada/clínica** (COR 1); profilaxia
antiepiléptica **sem crise não beneficia (COR 3: No Benefit)**.

## H-08 · Monitorização neuroinvasiva, PIC e edema (osmoterapia, DVE, corticoide)

**Localização:** §5.5 *Neuroinvasive Monitoring, ICP, and Edema Treatment*,
p. **e316**.
**População:** adultos com HIC espontânea / HIV.

> **rec. 1 · COR 1 · LOE B-NR** — "In patients with spontaneous ICH or IVH and
> hydrocephalus that is contributing to decreased level of consciousness,
> ventricular drainage should be performed to reduce mortality."

> **rec. 2 · COR 2b · LOE B-NR** — "In patients with moderate to severe
> spontaneous ICH or IVH with a reduced level of consciousness, ICP monitoring and
> treatment might be considered to reduce mortality and improve outcomes."

> **rec. 3 · COR 2b · LOE B-NR** — "In patients with spontaneous ICH, the efficacy
> of early prophylactic hyperosmolar therapy for improving outcomes is not well
> established."

> **rec. 4 · COR 2b · LOE C-LD** — "In patients with spontaneous ICH, bolus
> hyperosmolar therapy may be considered for transiently reducing ICP."

> **rec. 5 · COR 3: No Benefit · LOE B-R** — "In patients with spontaneous ICH,
> corticosteroids should not be administered for treatment of elevated ICP."

⚠️ **Achado (E-30):** osmoterapia em **bolus** serve para reduzir PIC
**transitoriamente (COR 2b)**; osmoterapia **profilática precoce não está bem
estabelecida**; **corticoide para PIC elevada NÃO deve ser administrado (COR 3: No
Benefit)** — reflexo clássico a ser bloqueado.

## H-09 · Tromboprofilaxia (TEV)

**Localização:** §5.3.3 *Thromboprophylaxis and Treatment of Thrombosis*,
p. **e310**.
**População:** adultos com HIC espontânea.

> **Prophylaxis · rec. 1 · COR 1 · LOE B-R** — "In nonambulatory patients with
> spontaneous ICH, intermittent pneumatic compression (IPC) starting on the day of
> diagnosis is recommended for DVT (DVT and pulmonary embolism [PE]) prophylaxis."

> **rec. 2 · COR 2a · LOE C-LD** — "In nonambulatory patients with spontaneous
> ICH, low-dose UFH or LMWH can be useful to reduce the risk for PE."

> **rec. 3 · COR 2b · LOE C-LD** — "In nonambulatory patients with spontaneous
> ICH, initiating low-dose UFH or LMWH prophylaxis at 24 to 48 hours from ICH onset
> may be reasonable to optimize the benefits of preventing thrombosis relative to
> the risk of HE."

> **rec. 4 · COR 3: No Benefit · LOE B-R** — "In nonambulatory patients with
> spontaneous ICH, graduated compression stockings of knee-high or thigh-high
> length alone are not beneficial for VTE prophylaxis."

⚠️ **Achado:** CPI (compressão pneumática intermitente) **desde o diagnóstico
(COR 1)**; meias de compressão graduada **isoladas não beneficiam (COR 3)**;
heparina profilática **24–48 h** do início (COR 2b).

## H-10 · Cirurgia — evacuação de HIC supratentorial (MIS) e craniotomia/craniectomia

**Localização:** §6.1.1 *MIS Evacuation of ICH*, p. **e318**; §6.1.3 *Craniotomy
for Supratentorial Hemorrhage*, p. **e322**; §6.2 *Craniectomy for ICH*, p. **e324**.
**População:** adultos com HIC supratentorial.

#### MIS supratentorial
> **§6.1.1 rec. 1 · COR 2a · LOE B-R** — "For patients with supratentorial ICH of
> >20- to 30-mL volume with GCS scores in the moderate range (5–12), minimally
> invasive hematoma evacuation with endoscopic or stereotactic aspiration with or
> without thrombolytic use can be useful to reduce mortality compared with medical
> management alone."

> **§6.1.1 rec. 2 · COR 2b · LOE B-R** — "For patients with supratentorial ICH of
> >20- to 30-mL volume with GCS scores in the moderate range (5–12) being
> considered for hematoma evacuation, it may be reasonable to select minimally
> invasive hematoma evacuation over conventional craniotomy to improve functional
> outcomes."

> **§6.1.1 rec. 3 · COR 2b · LOE B-R** — "For patients with supratentorial ICH of
> >20- to 30-mL volume with GCS scores in the moderate range (5–12), the
> effectiveness of minimally invasive hematoma evacuation with endoscopic or
> stereotactic aspiration with or without thrombolytic use to improve functional
> outcomes is uncertain."

#### Craniotomia supratentorial
> **§6.1.3 rec. 1 · COR 2b · LOE A** — "For most patients with spontaneous
> supratentorial ICH of moderate or greater severity, the usefulness of craniotomy
> for hemorrhage evacuation to improve functional outcomes or mortality is
> uncertain."

> **§6.1.3 rec. 2 · COR 2b · LOE C-LD** — "In patients with supratentorial ICH who
> are deteriorating, craniotomy for hematoma evacuation might be considered as a
> lifesaving measure."

#### Craniectomia descompressiva
> **§6.2 rec. 1 · COR 2b · LOE C-LD** — "In patients with supratentorial ICH who
> are in a coma, have large hematomas with significant midline shift, or have
> elevated ICP refractory to medical management, decompressive craniectomy with or
> without hematoma evacuation may be considered to reduce mortality."

> **§6.2 rec. 2 · COR 2b · LOE C-LD** — "In patients with supratentorial ICH who
> are in a coma, have large hematomas with significant midline shift, or have
> elevated ICP refractory to medical management, effectiveness of decompressive
> craniectomy with or without hematoma evacuation to improve functional outcomes
> is uncertain."

## H-11 · Cirurgia — hemorragia de fossa posterior (cerebelar) ⚠️ COR 1

**Localização:** §6.1.4 *Craniotomy for Posterior Fossa Hemorrhage*, p. **e323**.
**População:** adultos com HIC cerebelar.

> **rec. 1 · COR 1 · LOE B-NR** — "For patients with cerebellar ICH who are
> deteriorating neurologically, have brainstem compression and/or hydrocephalus
> from ventricular obstruction, or have cerebellar ICH volume ≥15 mL, immediate
> surgical removal of the hemorrhage with or without EVD is recommended in
> preference to medical management alone to reduce mortality."

⚠️⚠️ **Achado (E-30) — a síntese dizia ">15 mL"; o verbatim primário diz "≥15 mL".**
O verbatim vence. Esta é a **única recomendação cirúrgica COR 1** do documento na
HIC: cerebelar com deterioração, compressão de tronco/hidrocefalia OU **volume
≥15 mL → remoção cirúrgica imediata**. EVD isolada pode ser insuficiente (texto de
suporte, p. e323: "EVD alone may be insufficient when intracranial hypertension
impedes blood supply to the brainstem").

## H-14 · Prevenção secundária — PA e antitrombóticos

**Localização:** §9.1.2 *BP Management*, p. **e332**; §9.1.3 *Management of
Antithrombotic Agents*, p. **e333**; §9.1.4 *Management of Other Medications*,
p. **e335**.
**População:** sobreviventes de HIC espontânea (fase de prevenção).

#### Pressão arterial
> **§9.1.2 rec. 1 · COR 1 · LOE B-R** — "In patients with spontaneous ICH, BP
> control is recommended to prevent hemorrhage recurrence."

> **§9.1.2 rec. 2 · COR 2a · LOE B-NR** — "In patients with spontaneous ICH, it is
> reasonable to lower BP to an SBP of 130 mm Hg and diastolic BP (DBP) of 80 mm Hg
> for long-term management to prevent hemorrhage recurrence."

#### Antitrombóticos (retomada)
> **§9.1.3 rec. 1 · COR 2a · LOE C-LD** — "In patients with spontaneous ICH and
> conditions placing them at high risk of thromboembolic complications, for
> example, a mechanical valve or LVAD, early resumption of anticoagulation to
> prevent thromboembolic complications is reasonable."

> **§9.1.3 rec. 4 · COR 2b · LOE C-LD** — "In patients with AF and spontaneous ICH
> in whom the decision is made to restart anticoagulation, initiation of
> anticoagulation at ≈7 to 8 weeks after ICH may be considered after weighing
> specific patient characteristics to optimize the balance of risks and benefits."

#### Outros medicamentos
> **§9.1.4 rec. 2 · COR 3: Harm · LOE B-NR** — "In patients with spontaneous ICH,
> regular long-term use of nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drugs (NSAIDs) is
> potentially harmful because of the increased risk of ICH."

⚠️ **Achado:** alvo de longo prazo **PAS 130 / PAD 80 (COR 2a)**; reanticoagular na
FA **≈7–8 semanas (COR 2b)**; AINE crônico **potencialmente danoso (COR 3: Harm)**.
