## DOSE USUAL E TETO

> **Label A, 2.1, Tabela 1:** "Usual Adult Dose | 1 gram to 2 grams | Once a day or in
> equally divided doses every 12 hours | Should not exceed 4 grams*"

> **Label B:** "The usual adult daily dose is 1 to 2 grams given once a day (or in
> equally divided doses twice a day) depending on the type and severity of infection.
> The total daily dose should not exceed 4 grams."

> **Profilaxia cirúrgica (Label A, Tabela 1):** "Surgical prophylaxis | 1 gram I.V.
> once | ½ to 2 hours before surgery"

## ⚠️ FUNÇÃO RENAL — NÃO SE AJUSTA, E ISSO É CONTEÚDO

> **Label B, DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION:** "No dosage adjustment is necessary for
> patients with impairment of renal or hepatic function (see PRECAUTIONS)."

> **Label B, PRECAUTIONS:** "Ceftriaxone is excreted via both biliary and renal
> excretion… Therefore, patients with renal failure normally require no adjustment in
> dosage when usual doses of ceftriaxone are administered."

> **Label A, 12.3:** "…the pharmacokinetics of ceftriaxone were only minimally altered
> in elderly subjects and in patients with renal or hepatic impairment; therefore,
> dosage adjustments are not necessary for these patients with ceftriaxone dosages up
> to 2 grams per day."

## ⚠️ A EXCEÇÃO QUE IMPORTA NA UTI — RENAL **E** HEPÁTICA JUNTAS

> **Label A, 2.1 (nota de rodapé da Tabela 1):** "*Patients with hepatic impairment and
> significant renal impairment should not receive more than 2 grams per day of
> ceftriaxone."

> **Label A, 5.7:** "In patients with both hepatic impairment and significant renal
> disease, Ceftriaxone for Injection dosage should not exceed 2 grams per day."

> **Label B, PRECAUTIONS:** "…in patients with both hepatic dysfunction and significant
> renal disease, caution should be exercised and the ceftriaxone dosage should not
> exceed 2 g daily." · "In patients with both severe renal and hepatic dysfunction,
> close clinical monitoring for safety and efficacy is advised."

**É o cirrótico com injúria renal aguda — que não é caso raro em UTI.**

## ⚠️ E UMA TENSÃO DENTRO DO PRÓPRIO LABEL, QUE FICA DECLARADA

> **Label A, 5.3 (Neurological Adverse Reactions):** "Some cases occurred in patients
> with severe renal impairment who did not receive appropriate dosage adjustment.
> However, in other cases, neurological adverse reactions occurred in patients
> receiving an appropriate dosage adjustment. The neurological adverse reactions were
> reversible and resolved after discontinuation. … **Make appropriate dosage
> adjustments in patients with severe renal impairment.**"

O mesmo documento diz "não é necessário ajuste" na dosagem e "faça o ajuste apropriado
na disfunção renal grave" na seção de neurotoxicidade. **As duas entram.** Apagar uma
delas para deixar o app coerente seria inventar coerência que a fonte não tem.

## HEMODIÁLISE

> **Label B, PRECAUTIONS:** "Ceftriaxone is not removed by peritoneal- or hemodialysis.
> In patients undergoing dialysis no additional supplementary dosing is required
> following the dialysis."

> **Label A, 12.3:** "Ceftriaxone was not removed to any significant extent from the
> plasma by hemodialysis. In 6 of 26 dialysis patients, the elimination rate of
> ceftriaxone was markedly reduced, suggesting that plasma concentrations of
> ceftriaxone should be monitored in these patients to determine if dosage adjustments
> are necessary."

## ⚠️ CÁLCIO — SEGURANÇA DE ADMINISTRAÇÃO, NÃO DOSE

> **Label A, 5.2:** "Precipitation of ceftriaxone-calcium can occur when Ceftriaxone
> for Injection, USP is mixed with calcium-containing solutions in the same intravenous
> administration line. Ceftriaxone for Injection must not be administered
> simultaneously with calcium-containing intravenous solutions, including continuous
> calcium-containing infusions, such as parenteral nutrition via a Y-site. However, in
> patients other than neonates, Ceftriaxone for Injection, USP and calcium-containing
> solutions may be administered sequentially of one another if the infusion lines are
> thoroughly flushed between infusions with 0.9% Sodium Chloride Injection or D5W."

> **Label A, 2.3:** "Do not use diluents containing calcium, such as **Ringer's
> solution or Hartmann's solution**, to reconstitute … because a precipitate can form.
> STERILE WATER FOR INJECTION IS THE ONLY RECOMMENDED DILUENT FOR RECONSTITUTION."

> **Label B, CONTRAINDICATIONS:** "Ceftriaxone for injection is contraindicated in
> neonates (≤ 28 days) if they require (or are expected to require) treatment with
> calcium-containing IV solutions… because of the risk of precipitation of
> ceftriaxone-calcium."

⚠️ **O RINGER LACTATO ESTÁ NOMEADO NO LABEL**, e é a solução que mais circula em sala e
em emergência. Por isso esta informação é de beira de leito, não de bula guardada.
